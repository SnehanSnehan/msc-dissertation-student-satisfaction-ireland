# Comparing Satisfaction Between International and Irish Masters Taught Students in Irish Higher Education

MSc Data Science and Analytics dissertation, Department of Mathematics and Statistics, Maynooth University (August 2026).

**Author:** Snehan
**Supervisor:** Professor Catherine Hurley

**[Read the dissertation (PDF)](Snehan_MSc_Dissertation_Student_Satisfaction_Ireland.pdf)**

## Research question
Do international Masters Taught students in Irish higher education report different levels of overall satisfaction from Irish students, and what factors explain any differences?

## Key findings
- In 2019, Irish students were more satisfied than international students (83.0% vs 77.9%). By 2023 the gap had closed (81.5% for both groups).
- The gap closed because international students' satisfaction rose. Irish students' satisfaction stayed roughly flat.
- Domicile alone was not related to satisfaction. The relationship depended on study mode and field of study. In full-time STEM, international students were predicted to be about 7 percentage points more satisfied than Irish students.
- The international share of the Masters Taught cohort rose from 35.7% in 2019 to 52% in 2023.

## Methods
Binary logistic regression with a three-way interaction (domicile × study mode × field of study), ordinal logistic regression as a robustness check, model diagnostics (Hosmer-Lemeshow test, ROC/AUC), missing-data analysis, and a 2019 vs 2023 temporal comparison. Analysis in R.

## Data
StudentSurvey.ie (Irish Survey of Student Engagement), 2019 and 2023, obtained from the Irish Social Science Data Archive (ISSDA) under licence. **The data is not included in this repository and cannot be redistributed.** It can be requested from ISSDA. The findings are my own analysis and do not represent ISSDA or StudentSurvey.ie.
